JULIO privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026. This policy describes the Google Gmail identity integration in JULIO Financial Command Centre, a personal application operated by Trinity Burke.

Google data and permissions

With the owner’s consent, JULIO receives the authorised Google account’s email address, verified-email indicator and account identifier, plus OAuth access and refresh credentials. JULIO checks that the account is dad.built.digital@gmail.com. It requests only openid, basic email identity and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send.

JULIO uses the Gmail API to send single-use verification codes and fixed verification, revocation and test notifications from that address to itself. It records Google’s delivery message identifiers. It does not request inbox-reading, contacts or Google Drive permissions. JULIO does not obtain an existing ChatGPT Gmail connector’s credentials.

Purpose and limited use

Google data is used to connect the specified Gmail account, deliver identity messages, verify possession and maintain the related security audit. The integration does not send financial-message payloads or transaction amounts in these notifications. JULIO does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. The application does not provide an arbitrary-recipient email service.

JULIO’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Any operator access for support must be authorised by the owner and limited to the requested troubleshooting purpose.

Storage, protection and service providers

The private application uses ChatGPT Sites hosting and Cloudflare Workers with D1 storage. Refresh credentials and temporary OAuth verifiers are encrypted before storage. Application encryption and OAuth client secrets are held in server-side runtime secret storage. Access tokens are used transiently by the server. Verification codes are stored as keyed hashes, not plaintext.

Google processes OAuth authorisation and Gmail delivery. OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare provide the private application’s hosting, authentication and storage infrastructure. Public information pages may be hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure can process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses for its service operation. These public pages contain no dashboard data, OAuth tokens, sign-in forms, advertising scripts or application analytics.

Retention and deletion

JULIO retains the encrypted Gmail refresh credential while the connection is active. Disconnect Gmail removes the locally stored refresh credential and attempts to revoke the grant with Google. Expired temporary OAuth-state records are removed when a new connection begins. Email challenges, verification status, delivery metadata and immutable application audit history can remain after disconnect; disconnecting alone does not erase the ledger or audit history.

For a data-deletion request or questions about retained records, contact the operator at dad.built.digital@gmail.com. Requests require owner authentication and are handled manually; the application currently provides no automatic full-history deletion control. Records needed for security, integrity or applicable obligations may require separate handling.

Owner controls and revocation

The authenticated owner can disconnect Gmail, revoke the JULIO email verification, or reconnect through a new Google consent flow in the Identity section. The owner can also remove JULIO’s grant using Google Account’s third-party access controls. Revocation or expired credentials prevent subsequent sending until consent is renewed. Email ownership verification and active Gmail sending authorisation are separate states.

Changes and contact

Material changes to the integration’s Google data use will be reflected in this policy and, where required, a new consent request. Contact Trinity Burke at dad.built.digital@gmail.com.
